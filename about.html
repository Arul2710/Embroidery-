Our Story

Crafting Excellence Since 2018

Our studio was born from a simple belief: embroidery should be art. What started as a two-person operation with a single machine has grown into a premier embroidery studio serving fashion brands, streetwear labels, and corporations across the globe.

Our journey has been defined by an obsessive commitment to quality. We've invested in cutting-edge 12-head Tajima machines, built an in-house digitizing department, and assembled a team of master embroiderers who understand the subtle interplay of thread tension, stitch density, and fabric behavior.

Today, we produce over 50,000 embroidered pieces annually — each one bearing the hallmark of precision. From runway-ready fashion patches to rugged biker club insignias, our thread tells your story.
