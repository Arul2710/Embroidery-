FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you accept?+
We accept AI, EPS, PDF, PNG, JPG, SVG, and PSD files. Our digitizing team can work from virtually any format, including hand-drawn sketches and photographs.
What is the typical turnaround time?+
Standard turnaround is 5–7 business days for digitizing and 10–14 business days for production. Express 48-hour and 24-hour rush services are available at additional cost.
Do you offer samples before production?+
Yes, we provide physical samples for all orders over $200. This allows you to approve stitch quality, color accuracy, and placement before full production begins.
What is the minimum order quantity?+
There is no minimum for custom patches — we accept single-piece orders. For bulk uniform embroidery, minimums vary by complexity. Contact us for details.
Do you ship internationally?+
Absolutely. We ship to over 40 countries worldwide via FedEx, DHL, and UPS. International delivery typically takes 5–10 business days depending on destination.
